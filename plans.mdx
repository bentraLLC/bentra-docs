Every organization belongs to an **account**. The account has a **plan**, which sets the limits and features for each of its organizations. Enterprise contracts can change any limit.

| | Free | Team | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organizations per account | 1 | 1 | 3 | Unlimited |
| Users (requesters are free) | 3 | 10 | 100 | Unlimited |
| Workflows | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Runners | 1 | 3 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Runner pools | 1 | 1 | 2 | Unlimited |
| Enabled schedules | 2 | 20 | 50 | Unlimited |
| Runs per month | 300 | 5,000 | 25,000 | Unlimited |
| Run log retention (days) | 3 | 14 | 30 | 30 |
| [Request portal](/guides/request-portal) | | Included | Included | Included |
| SSO, SCIM, audit logs | | | | Included |

Bentra can raise the runner and runner pool limits for Team and Business accounts on request. Upgrade, change or cancel a plan in **Settings → Billing**. See [Billing](/guides/billing).

## How limits behave

- **Limits only stop new things.** Nothing you already have is disabled or deleted when you're over a limit.
- **Users** count owners, admins and members. Requesters (portal-only) are free and unlimited.
- **Workflows**: archived workflows don't count. Restoring one counts again.
- **Runners** count the runners connected across all pools. A runner over the limit is refused until another one stops. See [Install a runner](/runners/install).
- **Runs per month** reset on the 1st (UTC). At the limit, forms, the API, the portal, schedules and manual triggers stop accepting new runs until the month resets.
- **Usage**: **Settings → Billing** shows the plan, the limits and this month's usage (workflows, runs, users, runners and pools) to owners and admins. The Workflows and Runs lists show a banner from 80% of a limit.

## When a plan ends

- **Cancelled subscription**: the plan stays until the end of the period you paid for, then the organization moves to Free. Billing shows the date beforehand.
- **Failed payment**: the plan stays while the payment is retried, and Billing shows a warning.
- **Unpaid or ended subscription**: the organization moves to Free and keeps all its data. Free limits then apply to anything new.
- **Contract with an end date** (Enterprise): Billing warns 30 days ahead. For 7 days after the end date everything keeps working. Then the organization is read-only until day 30, after which only owners and admins can sign in, to **Settings → Billing**, and API, MCP and runner tokens stop working. Data is kept.

## Organizations and their IDs

- Anyone can sign up and create an organization. It starts on **Free** on its own account.
- **Free and Team allow one organization at a time.** To start over, an owner deletes the organization (**Settings → Organization**), which also removes its account, and then creates a new one. An organization with an active subscription can't be deleted until the subscription is set to cancel. See [Organization settings](/guides/organization-settings).
- **Business and Enterprise accounts** add organizations from **Settings → Billing → New organization** (owners and admins), or with `POST account/organizations/`. New organizations join the same account and plan.
- **Names don't have to be unique.** An organization's URL ID (`/orgs/<id>/`, the API's `slug`) is 12 random characters generated at creation. It never comes from the name and never changes. **Settings → Organization** shows it as *URL ID*.
