---
title: "Bentra"
description: "Turn infrastructure tasks into forms and jobs your teams can run, approve and follow."
---

Bentra runs infrastructure workflows. A workflow is either a **request**, where people fill in a form and it optionally goes through approval before it runs, or a **job**, run manually, by API or on a schedule. Steps run on runners in your own infrastructure.
